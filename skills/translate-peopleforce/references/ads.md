# Ads-only checks — fixed canvas and mixed

Checks 12 and 13, continuing `SKILL.md`'s eleven, for any job step 1 typed as **fixed
canvas** or **mixed**. A flowing-text job never runs them.

- [ ] **12 · Report the length; the number is information, not a target** (scoring it
      produces worse translations, not shorter ones). Give the shortest faithful line and
      state its character count beside the budget — or beside the source line's count,
      with the budget named unknown. Characters, not words: a box cares about rendered
      width. Buy space from layout and phrasing, never from meaning or a **conditional**
      glossary term; where variants are **interchangeable**, take the shorter one and hold
      it across the set. An overrun allows at most two further complete attempts at the
      line, per line — a third would be needed means stop and report how far over, with
      the attempt count in the handover. That report is the deliverable, not a failure.
      Articles have no budget at all.

- [ ] **13 · A figure holds its position in every language.** Stat blocks and ad sets are
      read as a column across language versions: if the figure opens the source line it
      opens all of them, and if it closes, it closes everywhere. A preposition in front of
      the figure still counts as leading. Where holding the position would force
      ungrammatical word order, the language wins: move it and name the line that broke
      rank in the handover, with its reason. In flowing text the figure sits where the
      sentence needs it. Read the column, not the line.
